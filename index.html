Welcome Aboard

Nestled near the Delaware coast, The Wheelhouse blends modern American cuisine with the charm of a classic seaside tavern. Whether you're joining us after a day at the beach or celebrating with friends, every meal is crafted with fresh ingredients and local inspiration.


Our menu features fresh seafood, premium steaks, artisan flatbreads, seasonal vegetables, and signature desserts—all paired with an extensive wine list and creative cocktails.
